FAQ

Answers for a confident first step.

What services do you offer?

We provide landscaping design, lawn installation, maintenance plans, planting, irrigation, outdoor lighting, tree care, garden cleaning and hardscape coordination.

Do you work on commercial projects?

Yes, we design and maintain residential, hospitality, office, and multi-property landscapes that need both beauty and practicality.

How long do landscaping projects take?

Project timing varies by scope, but most residential transformations take between two and eight weeks depending on planting, hardscaping and site conditions.

Can you help with sustainable water use?

Absolutely. We recommend drought-tolerant planting, irrigation planning, mulching and design strategies that reduce ongoing water use.
